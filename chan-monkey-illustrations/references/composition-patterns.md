# Composition patterns

Pick one structure per image. Never name it on the image.

## Structure types

**Pipeline** — input → transform → output (content production, AI workflows, automations).
Input on the left, the monkey operating a strange machine in the middle, output on the
right, one orange flow line.

**System slice** — 3–5 parts of a larger system (sources, filters, a store, an agent).
Draw only those parts; the monkey works the one that matters.

**Before / after** — messy vs tidy, manual vs automated, scattered vs gathered.
Left and right halves, one orange arrow between; the monkey's mood changes (lost → cool).

**States** — 2–4 small vignettes of the same monkey in different states (stuck, trying,
flowing). One short label each.

**Concept machine** — one big odd object that *is* the idea (a press, a sieve, a loom, a
well), a little going in, one thing coming out. The memorable one.

**Layers** — a stack the monkey is building, propping up, or climbing; not a formal pyramid.

**Route** — a winding path with few stops; the monkey walks it, drags something along it,
or uses its tail to hook the next stop.

**Strip** — 2–4 panels, one action each, no panel borders heavier than a light line.

**Loop** — something that comes back around (feedback, iteration, compounding); the
monkey keeps it turning, often with its tail as the crank or the belt.

## Inventing the metaphor

Start from the article, never from the examples.

1. **Turn the abstract claim into a physical verb:** jam, leak, weigh, sort, ferment, fold,
   unpack, compress, sprout, tangle, untie, hand over, bounce back, compound.
2. **Turn the system into a low-tech object:** a jar, a sieve, a pulley, a drawer, a mail
   slot, a loom, a kettle, a scale, a ladder, a garden hose, a typewriter, a seed tray,
   a crate, a periscope, a key ring, a weathervane. Use 1–2, not a pile.
3. **Give the verb to the monkey:** it cranks, hooks with its tail, threads, pours, stamps,
   prunes, waters, measures, plugs, swaps, catches, balances, ties.

Check: does the monkey's action *say* the idea? If the monkey were removed, would the
picture lose its meaning? It should.

## Monkey-native moves

Lean on what only this character has:

- **The tail** as a third hand: holding the pen while both hands carry; hooking the next
  step before the current one is done; coiled round a lever as a spring.
- **The curl** as a sprout: an idea literally growing from its head (draw the new growth in
  orange or violet, never ink on ink).
- **Climbing / swinging:** jumping between options, hanging from a dependency, swinging
  across a gap the article talks about.
- **Peeking:** the head rising over the edge of a box, a doc, a wall — the portfolio's own
  "peeking monkey" pose.
- **Bananas, sparingly:** at most once per article, and only when it's the funniest true
  metaphor (a reward, a payoff). Not a default prop.

## Anti-repetition

Within one article, no two images share a structure type *and* a main object. Across
articles, do not reuse the compositions in `assets/examples/` unless the user explicitly
asks to redo one of them; a similar idea gets a different object and a different action.
